“The team reviewed feedback from customers who completed onboarding this month. Several customers said the setup instructions were difficult to follow. We discussed creating a short onboarding video and adding a checklist to the welcome email. Some team members preferred the video, while others thought improving the written guide would be faster. No decision was made about which approach to take. The team also considered scheduling a weekly onboarding Q&A session, but we did not assign anyone to organize it. Current onboarding activities will continue as usual while we evaluate the options.”
